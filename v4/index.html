Explore all eleven jobs.

The walkthrough above follows one opportunity to prove the mechanism. These are the eleven jobs the same team member holds, day to day.

01Missed-call text-back▾
A missed call gets a branded text in about nine seconds, at any hour. The caller can ask to be called back or book directly, and the exchange lands in the Agency App with the caller's number and context.
02Persistent lead follow-up▾
New inquiries are answered in seconds, then followed up on an approved cadence: dense while the prospect is deciding, spacing out over the first month, then continuing on the approved longer-term schedule. The sequence stops when they reply, book, decline, opt out, or need licensed judgment.
03Online booking and reminders▾
Prospects book themselves into real open slots on the right producer's calendar, straight from a text or the website. Reminders go out before the appointment so booked time turns into held appointments.
04Website and supported-channel conversations▾
One conversational layer, trained on your agency's approved information, answers on your website chat and supported messaging channels. It qualifies, books, and logs the conversation, and hands anything needing judgment to your team.
05Unified Agency App and reporting▾
Calls, texts, emails, bookings, reactivations and reviews report into one view, on your phone and on the desktop. Every item shows what happened, who owns the next step, and whether your approval is needed.
06Database and renewal reactivation▾
On agency-approved rules and eligible records, Regesta works the book you already own: unclosed quotes, upcoming renewals, appropriate lapsed customers, and suitable single-policy conversations. Responsive opportunities are booked and assigned to a person.
07Review requests▾
After a completed customer outcome, a neutral review request goes out by text or email, with a polite reminder if the customer forgets. The sequence stops when they act.
08Review-response drafting▾
Every review gets a specific drafted response in the agency's voice, without exposing private policy information. Responses to sensitive or low-rated reviews can be held for owner approval before anything is published.
09Optional conversion-focused website▾
If you want it, a fast agency website built around getting a quote started and a conversation opened. Every other job plugs into the site you already have, so the rebuild is an option, not a condition.
10Voice callback▾
Where permitted and configured, Regesta can ring the caller back, find out what they need, and either book the next step or connect them with your team while they are on the line.
11After-hours email triage▾
Customer emails that arrive after hours get an approved initial response in the same thread, missing details are requested, and the completed conversation is assigned to the right person for the morning.
